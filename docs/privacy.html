Privacy Policy — Still

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Still does not collect any data. It has no analytics, no accounts, no telemetry, and no server of its own. Nothing you browse is recorded, and nothing leaves your browser.

What is stored

Two pieces of information are saved locally in your browser's extension storage, both of them settings you chose:

  • Whether Still is turned on or off.
  • The list of websites you have chosen to allow animations on.

This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted anywhere, and it is removed when you uninstall the extension.

What Still does on the pages you visit

To stop animations, Still runs a script on the pages you open. That script inspects the page's images and elements and modifies them so they do not animate. Page content is examined only in your browser, only while the page is open, and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Network requests Still makes

Still sometimes needs to know whether an image is animated when the URL alone does not say. To find out, it requests the first few kilobytes of that image — the same image the page was already loading — and checks the file for animation markers. These requests go only to the site hosting the image, carry no cookies or credentials, and are never sent to us or to any third party.

Permissions

  • host_permissions (all sites) — motion triggers are not limited to a known list of sites, so the script must be able to run wherever you read.
  • storage — saves the two settings listed above.
  • activeTab — lets the toolbar popup show the current site's name and its per-site toggle.
  • declarativeNetRequest — a static, shipped ruleset that blocks one known class of auto-spinning preview videos.
  • webRequest — reads response content-type headers for image requests to identify animated GIFs served without a file extension. Used for observation only; nothing is recorded.

Children

Still is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.
